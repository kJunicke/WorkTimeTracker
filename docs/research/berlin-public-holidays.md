# Gesetzliche Feiertage — Land Berlin (2026–2028)

Research note for the `feiertagskalender` feature: which days are statutory public
holidays in Berlin, on which exact dates, and how far into the future that list is
actually confirmed by a primary source.

**All sources accessed 2026-08-10.**

Primary sources used:

- **Statute (authoritative):** Gesetz über die Sonn- und Feiertage vom 28. Oktober 1954
  (GVBl. S. 615), § 1 Abs. 1 — Berlin's Landesrecht portal entry
  [1 FeiertG BE](https://gesetze.berlin.de/bsbe/document/jlr-FeiertGBEV10P1).
- **Amending laws, verbatim, from the Gesetz- und Verordnungsblatt für Berlin (GVBl.):**

  - [Viertes Gesetz zur Änderung des Gesetzes über die Sonn- und Feiertage vom 10. Juli 2024, GVBl. 80. Jg. Nr. 28 vom 20.7.2024, S. 460](https://pardok.parlament-berlin.de/starweb/adis/citat/VT/19/gvbl/g24280460.pdf)

## 1. What the statute names

[§ 1 Abs. 1 FeiertG BE](https://gesetze.berlin.de/bsbe/document/jlr-FeiertGBEV10P1)
lists the *allgemeinen Feiertage* as a numbered list. Its current shape (Nr. 1–11) is the
1954 list as amended twice:

**2019 amendment** ([GVBl. 2019 Nr. 3, S. 22](https://www.berlin.de/sen/justiz/service/gesetze-und-verordnungen/2019/ausgabe-nr-3-vom-6-2-2019-s-21-32.pdf), accessed 2026-08-10) inserted the Frauentag as a new Nr. 2:

> "Nach Nummer 1 wird folgende neue Nummer 2 eingefügt: „2. der Frauentag (8. März)“ …
> Die bisherigen Nummern 2 bis 9 werden die Nummern 3 bis 10."

The same law added a Nr. 11 for **der 8. Mai 2020** (75. Jahrestag der Befreiung) and, in
its own Artikel 2, repealed that Nr. 11 again with effect from **9. Mai 2020**. So the
2020 Tag der Befreiung was a self-expiring one-off and is *not* in force today.

**2024 amendment** ([Viertes Gesetz vom 10. Juli 2024, GVBl. 2024 Nr. 28, S. 460](https://pardok.parlament-berlin.de/starweb/adis/citat/VT/19/gvbl/g24280460.pdf), accessed 2026-08-10) repeated the same self-expiring trick, this time for two dates. Artikel 1 appended:

> "11. der 8. Mai 2025 (80. Jahrestag der Befreiung vom Nationalsozialismus und der
> Beendigung des Zweiten Weltkriegs in Europa).
> 12. der 17. Juni 2028 (75. Jahrestag des Aufstandes vom 17. Juni 1953)."

Artikel 2 repeals Nr. 11 and renumbers Nr. 12 → Nr. 11; Artikel 3 then repeals that Nr. 11
as well. Artikel 4 Inkrafttreten:

> "(2) Artikel 2 tritt am 9. Mai 2025 in Kraft. (3) Artikel 3 tritt am 18. Juni 2028 in Kraft."

**Consequence, and this is the load-bearing finding:**

- The 8 May "Tag der Befreiung" one-off applied **only in 2020 and 2025**. It is
  *not* a holiday in 2026, 2027 or 2028 — the statutory Nr. 11 for 8 May 2025 was
  removed by operation of law on 9 May 2025.
- 8 May remains a **Gedenktag** under [§ 3 Abs. 2 FeiertG BE](https://www.berlin.de/sen/justiz/service/gesetze-und-verordnungen/2019/ausgabe-nr-3-vom-6-2-2019-s-21-32.pdf) (as recast in 2019) — a day of remembrance, **not** a work-free Feiertag. Same for 18 March, 17 June (in ordinary years), 9 November, Volkstrauertag and Totensonntag. None of these are holidays.
- A **one-off holiday on 17 June 2028** *is* currently in force in § 1 Abs. 1 Nr. 11 and
  expires on 18 June 2028. Confirmed independently by berlin.de:
  [One-off public holiday](https://www.berlin.de/en/news/9188766-5559700-oneoff-public-holiday-on-8-may-2025.en.html) (accessed 2026-08-10).

No **Fünftes Änderungsgesetz** exists as of 2026-08-10 (searched the Berlin GVBl and
parliamentary record; only the Drittes and Viertes Gesetz appear).

## 2. The recurring holiday list

Per [§ 1 Abs. 1 FeiertG BE](https://gesetze.berlin.de/bsbe/document/jlr-FeiertGBEV10P1) and the
[Senatsverwaltung für Inneres listing](https://www.berlin.de/sen/inneres/buerger-und-staat/verfassungs-und-verwaltungsrecht/artikel.1435639.php)
(accessed 2026-08-10), which names the legal basis as *"Artikel 2 des Einigungsvertrags,
Artikel 35 der Verfassung von Berlin und § 1 des Gesetzes über die Sonn- und Feiertage"*,
Berlin has **ten** recurring statutory holidays plus all Sundays:

| # | German name | Rule | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Neujahrstag | 1 January | fixed |
| 2 | Frauentag | 8 March | fixed (Berlin-only; since 2019) |
| 3 | Karfreitag | Easter Sunday − 2 days | Easter-derived |
| 4 | Ostermontag | Easter Sunday + 1 day | Easter-derived |
| 5 | 1. Mai / Tag der Arbeit | 1 May | fixed |
| 6 | Christi Himmelfahrt | Easter Sunday + 39 days | Easter-derived |
| 7 | Pfingstmontag | Easter Sunday + 50 days | Easter-derived |
| 8 | Tag der Deutschen Einheit | 3 October | fixed |
| 9 | Erster Weihnachtstag | 25 December | fixed |
| 10 | Zweiter Weihnachtstag | 26 December | fixed |

Berlin has **no** Fronleichnam, Reformationstag, Allerheiligen, Buß- und Bettag or
Mariä Himmelfahrt. Easter Sunday and Pfingstsonntag are themselves not listed in § 1 —
they are covered as Sundays.

Easter Sunday follows the Gregorian computus (first Sunday after the first
ecclesiastical full moon on or after 21 March): **2026-04-05**, **2027-03-28**, **2028-04-16**.

## 3. Dates

Dates for 2026 and 2027 taken verbatim from
[berlin.de — Public Holidays & School Holidays](https://www.berlin.de/en/tourism/travel-information/1887651-2862820-public-holidays-school-holidays.en.html)
(accessed 2026-08-10). 2028 dates are derived from the § 1 rules and cross-checked against
the two APIs below; berlin.de does **not** publish 2028 dates on that page.

| Holiday | 2026 | weekday | 2027 | weekday | 2028 (derived) | weekday |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Neujahrstag | 2026-01-01 | Thu | 2027-01-01 | Fri | 2028-01-01 | Sat |
| Frauentag | 2026-03-08 | **Sun** | 2027-03-08 | Mon | 2028-03-08 | Wed |
| Karfreitag | 2026-04-03 | Fri | 2027-03-26 | Fri | 2028-04-14 | Fri |
| Ostermontag | 2026-04-06 | Mon | 2027-03-29 | Mon | 2028-04-17 | Mon |
| 1. Mai | 2026-05-01 | Fri | 2027-05-01 | Sat | 2028-05-01 | Mon |
| Christi Himmelfahrt | 2026-05-14 | Thu | 2027-05-06 | Thu | 2028-05-25 | Thu |
| Pfingstmontag | 2026-05-25 | Mon | 2027-05-17 | Mon | 2028-06-05 | Mon |
| *17. Juni (one-off)* | — | | — | | **2028-06-17** | Sat |
| Tag der Deutschen Einheit | 2026-10-03 | Sat | 2027-10-03 | **Sun** | 2028-10-03 | Tue |
| Erster Weihnachtstag | 2026-12-25 | Fri | 2027-12-25 | Sat | 2028-12-25 | Mon |
| Zweiter Weihnachtstag | 2026-12-26 | Sat | 2027-12-26 | **Sun** | 2028-12-26 | Tue |

Since today is 2026-08-10, the **remaining 2026 holidays** are: 2026-10-03, 2026-12-25,
2026-12-26.

**No substitute day.** German law grants no replacement when a Feiertag falls on a
Sunday or Saturday. 2026-03-08 (Sun), 2027-10-03 (Sun) and 2027-12-26 (Sun) simply
coincide with Sundays; 2026-10-03, 2026-12-26, 2027-05-01, 2027-12-25 and 2028-01-01
and 2028-06-17 fall on Saturdays. For a work-time tracker this matters: a holiday on a
non-working day yields no time credit and must not consume a vacation day either.

## 4. Cross-check against machine-readable sources

Both APIs were queried on 2026-08-10 for `DE-BE`, 2026-01-01 through 2028-12-31:

- [openholidaysapi.org](https://openholidaysapi.org/PublicHolidays?countryIsoCode=DE&subdivisionCode=DE-BE&languageIsoCode=DE&validFrom=2026-01-01&validTo=2028-12-31)
- [feiertage-api.de](https://feiertage-api.de/api/?jahr=2026&nur_land=BE) (also `jahr=2027`, `jahr=2028`)

**Every date matches the statute and berlin.de exactly, for all three years, including
the 17 June 2028 one-off.** No date discrepancies found. Only naming differs:

| Source | Frauentag | 17.06.2028 |
|---|---|---|
| Statute (§ 1 Abs. 1) | "der Frauentag (8. März)" | "der 17. Juni 2028 (75. Jahrestag des Aufstandes vom 17. Juni 1953)" |
| openholidaysapi.org | "Internationaler Frauentag" | "75-jährige Jubiläum des Volksaufstands in der DDR" |
| feiertage-api.de | "Frauentag" | "17. Juni 2028" (hinweis: "Einmaliger Feiertag, 75. Jahrestag des Aufstandes vom 17. Juni 1953") |

openholidaysapi additionally flags `nationwide: false` for Frauentag and 17 June 2028 and
`true` for the other eight — consistent with Frauentag being a Berlin-only holiday.

Neither API is a primary source. Both agree with the statute here, so they are safe as a
seed for test fixtures, but the statute governs.

## 5. Verified-through horizon

**Verified through 2028-12-31**, with the following precision:

- **The recurring ten-holiday list is verified as of 2026-08-10** — i.e. that is the
  version of § 1 Abs. 1 FeiertG BE in force today. It has no expiry; it holds until an
  amending law changes it. There is no "confirmed until" date for a permanent statute.
- **Exact dates are published by berlin.de through 2027-12-31 only.** 2028 dates in this
  note are computed from the § 1 rules, not read off an official calendar.
- **One-off holidays are fully enumerated through 2028-06-18**, because the Viertes
  Gesetz vom 10. Juli 2024 is the only in-force source of one-offs and its last operative
  clause takes effect 2028-06-18. Beyond that date, no one-off exists in current law.
- **Nothing in Berlin law or on berlin.de reaches past 2028.** No holiday is scheduled or
  announced for 2029+. Anything after 2028-06-18 is the recurring ten only.

**What would invalidate this note:**

1. A **Fünftes (or later) Änderungsgesetz** adding, removing or renaming a holiday —
   e.g. a further one-off Gedenk-Feiertag. Berlin has now done this twice (2019 for
   8 May 2020, 2024 for 8 May 2025 and 17 June 2028), typically **one to two years in
   advance**, so a new one-off for 2027 or 2028 could still be enacted. Re-check the
   [GVBl. index](https://www.berlin.de/sen/justiz/service/gesetze-und-verordnungen/artikel.261829.php)
   for any "… Gesetz zur Änderung des Gesetzes über die Sonn- und Feiertage".
2. A federal change to the Einigungsvertrag / a new bundeseinheitlicher Feiertag.
3. Nothing else. Easter-derived dates are pure computation and need no re-verification.

**Recommended re-check cadence:** annually, and specifically before 2028, since 17 June
2028 is easy to miss and applies to Berlin alone.

## 6. Note on source accessibility

[gesetze.berlin.de](https://gesetze.berlin.de/bsbe/document/jlr-FeiertGBEV10P1) is a
JavaScript single-page app (juris GmbH) and returns no statutory text to plain HTTP
fetches — the consolidated § 1 could not be transcribed directly from it, and it carries a
`tdm-reservation` meta tag. The verbatim statutory wording in this note therefore comes
from the **GVBl. PDFs of the amending laws**, which are equally primary and are
machine-readable. Version identifiers seen in the portal (`…FeiertGBEV7P1`,
`V9P1`, `V10P1`) are consecutive consolidated versions of § 1; `V10` is the one linked as
current, consistent with the 2025-05-09 auto-repeal being the most recent change.
